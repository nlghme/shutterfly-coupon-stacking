## The fine print that actually decides whether you get paid

This is the part most coupon roundups skip, and it's where cash back quietly dies. Four rules matter.

**1. App orders don't earn cash back.** BeFrugal's terms state it outright — purchases made in the Shutterfly app are excluded — and other portals' terms point the same direction for app-based orders generally. Place the order in a desktop or mobile browser after clicking through the portal.

**2. The last click wins.** Cash back attributes to whichever portal link you clicked most recently before checkout. If you click through TopCashback, then wander off to a coupon site, then come back to Shutterfly directly, you just gave your cash back to nobody. Same problem with browser extensions: a Honey or Rakuten popup that fires at checkout can hijack the tracking. Disable other shopping extensions, or decline their popups, before completing the order.

**3. Codes from outside the portal can void the cash back.** Portals publish codes they're authorized to display, and using those is safe. Using a code scraped from an aggregator is a gamble — TopCashback's terms exclude purchases made with codes not found on its site, and "coupons may reduce rate" is a shared rule across most portals, not a TopCashback quirk. The safe pattern: use the portal's own displayed codes, or use no code at all.

**4. Whole categories of Shutterfly purchases earn nothing.** Across portal terms, the recurring exclusions are:

- Shutterfly gift cards, whether buying or redeeming
- Prepaid or annual savings plans
- Purchases made through Tiny Prints, Lifetouch, Groovebook, BorrowLenses, or Spoonflower
- Physical-store returns, exchanges, or adjustments
- Resale and bulk orders

None of these are obscure edge cases. Buying a Shutterfly gift card for a family member and expecting cash back on it is one of the most common ways people lose a payout. And note the cash back base again: product price only, tax and shipping excluded.

One more Shutterfly-specific wrinkle: if you're buying 4x6 prints and need them fast, Shutterfly offers 1-hour pickup at Walgreens, CVS, and Target — select the 1-hour pickup option on the order page. Matte finish and back-of-photo messaging aren't available for that service, and portal cash back on pickup orders shouldn't be assumed.
